## Key Takeaways — Hands-on: Basic 3D Medical Image Annotation using 3D Slicer

* The hands-on session demonstrated the complete end-to-end workflow for 3D medical image annotation, from data loading to exporting segmentation masks.

* 3D Slicer is a powerful open-source platform for visualizing and annotating volumetric medical imaging data such as CT and MRI scans.

* Built-in Sample Data in 3D Slicer is sufficient for learning and practicing core annotation workflows without relying on external datasets.

* Correct loading of 3D volumetric data is verified by navigating through axial, sagittal, and coronal views.

* Understanding window and level adjustments is essential for clear visualization of anatomical structures and accurate ROI identification.

* Manual ROI segmentation using the Segment Editor module is a foundational skill for radiomics and AI-based medical image analysis.

* Slice-wise annotation with paint and erase tools helps build spatial understanding of anatomical structures across volumes.

* Annotating a limited number of consecutive slices is sufficient for learning the segmentation workflow before scaling to full organ or lesion annotation.

* 3D visualization of segmented regions is crucial for checking anatomical continuity and identifying segmentation artifacts.

* Exporting segmentation masks as labelmaps in NIfTI format (.nii / .nii.gz) enables compatibility with downstream radiomics and AI pipelines.

* Manual segmentation serves as a gold standard reference for training, validating, and benchmarking automated AI models.

* This annotation workflow forms the standard preprocessing stage in radiomics feature extraction and AI-based segmentation and classification tasks.

* Mastery of basic annotation tools is essential before progressing to automated or semi-automated segmentation methods such as MONAI Label.

* Overall, the session provided practical, hands-on experience in creating high-quality annotated datasets, which are critical for reliable medical AI development.
